HOW TO PLAY

🎮

Desktop Controls

  • A / ← Move Left
  • D / → Move Right
  • W / ↑ Jump
  • J Punch
  • K Kick
  • L Special Ability
  • Shift Dodge
🏆

Tournament Rules

  • 11 levels, 1v1 fights each
  • Best-of-3 rounds per match
  • Win 2 rounds to win the match
  • Lose the match = Game Over
  • Clear all 11 levels = Champion!
⚡

Combat Tips

  • Punch: Short range, fast
  • Kick: Long range, knockback
  • Special: Unique per fighter
  • Dodge: Temporary invincibility
  • Watch the cooldown timer!
⏱

Round Rules

  • 60-second timer per round
  • Health hits 0 = round loss
  • Time's up: higher HP wins
  • Round 3 if 1–1 tie
